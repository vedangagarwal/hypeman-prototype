HypeMan · UI v0.7 · latest design · clickable prototype · for developers

Every screen, connected

Start anywhere. Links, nav and buttons take you between screens. The pill at bottom-left switches desktop and mobile or brings you back here. Anything not designed yet shows a short notice instead of a dead link.

Not in this prototype: Auth (A1–A4), onboarding (O1–O5) and status screens (S1–S3). They exist only in Figma. Older duplicates are kept at the bottom under “Put aside” for you to check, nothing was deleted.
